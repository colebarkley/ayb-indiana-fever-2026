📋 COACH’S NOTES & PLAY RULES (HALF-COURT & BASELINE OOB)+

1. HALF-COURT OFFENSE: DOWNTOWN

Downtown (Half-Court Only): Elbows (4 & 5) screen down for blocks (2 & 3), who pop wide to the 3pt wings while 4 & 5 seal the blocks.

2. BASELINE OUT OF BOUNDS: UPTOWN & CROSSTOWN

Uptown (Baseline OOB): Blocks (2 & 3) screen up so elbows (4 & 5) dive to the blocks. Crosstown (Baseline OOB): Ball-side 5 & 3 screen across the lane for weak-side teammates 4 & 2.

3. CONTINUING MOTION & SMART PASSING

After the entry pass, screen away or replace the vacated low block. Wing-to-top reversals and ball-side block feeds are safe; avoid long cross-court skip passes or throwing from down on the block all the way out to the top of the key.
